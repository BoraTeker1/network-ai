# Concierge Runbook — do it by hand for 5 people

**Why:** before automating anything further, prove the *value* is real. You manually run
the whole workflow for 5 real job seekers. If hand-delivered outreach doesn't get them
replies, no amount of product polish will. If it does, you've found the core value — and
5 warm potential first customers.

**Who:** 5 people from your interviews / landing page who said "do mine now." Their
willingness to give you their time and résumé is itself a strong signal.

**Time:** ~30–45 min per person. Use the **real app** where it helps — that's the point.

---

## The run (per person)

1. **Intake (5 min).** Get their résumé + one sentence: "What role and region are you
   targeting?" Save the résumé in the app's Profile so matching is résumé-aware.

2. **Shortlist roles (10 min).** Use `/opportunities` (refresh live sources). Pick the
   **3 best-fit, actually-applicable** roles (Strong/Possible label, junior, remote/EU).
   Sanity-check each on the company page — you're the quality filter here.

3. **Find a person for each (10 min).** Use the outreach copilot's "Who to contact"
   links. For each role, find **one** real recruiter or engineer (name + LinkedIn).
   Paste them in. *(This is the step you're testing for compliant feasibility at scale.)*

4. **Draft (5 min).** Generate the bilingual outreach draft for each. Edit so it's
   genuinely good — honest, specific, low-pressure.

5. **Hand off (5 min).** Send the person all 3: role + contact + ready-to-send draft.
   **They** send it from their own LinkedIn/email. (Never send on their behalf.)

6. **Track.** Log it. Follow up in 5–7 days: did they send? Any replies?

---

## Per-person log

```
Name / target role + region:
Roles shortlisted (3):
Contacts found (name + how long it took each):
Hardest part of the run for YOU (operator):
Did they actually send?  3 / __
Replies within 7 days:  __
Their reaction after seeing the drafts (quote):
Would they pay to keep doing this themselves? (read, 0–5):
```

---

## What each part teaches you

- **Roles step:** Is your live-jobs coverage good enough to find 3 real fits per person?
  If you struggle, that's a Phase-2 gap (more sources), surfaced by real use.
- **Contacts step:** How long does finding one real person take *you*, by hand, with the
  search links? If it's fast and reliable → the "reframe" flow is enough. If it's
  painful → that's the evidence you'd need before paying for Hunter.io (Phase 3 Option B).
- **Replies:** The actual product value. **≥ 2 of 5 getting a reply in a week** is a
  strong "the value is real" signal. Zero replies across 5 → the outreach itself isn't
  landing; fix that before anything else.
- **Their reaction:** People who, after a free concierge run, ask "can I keep doing this
  myself / when's the tool ready" are your **first paying customers.** Note their names.

---

## Feed it back

- Add every reply, every "this is great," and every operator-pain-point to the Day-6
  scoreboard and the Day-7 decision.
- The best quotes become your landing-page testimonials and outreach copy.
- The operator pain points become your **real** product roadmap — grounded in use, not
  guesses.
